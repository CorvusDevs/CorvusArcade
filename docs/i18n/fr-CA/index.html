Corvus Arcade

Run Windows games on your Apple Silicon Mac. A native launcher with its own bundled engine, Wine 11 with DXMT and Apple's D3DMetal. No CrossOver, no Terminal. Pick a game, press Play.

Free Download Free · macOS 26+ · Apple Silicon only Signed with an Apple Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

What runs today

Battle.net and Overwatch, DRM-free GOG installers, and games that ship as a plain archive are all tested and working. Games protected by anti-cheat will not run, and DirectX 12-only titles are still unproven. Corvus Arcade tells you which is which before you install.

Features

A launcher, not a toolkit

One-click Overwatch

A curated setup builds a clean container, installs Battle.net, and launches straight toward a match. No configuration.

Your engine, bundled

Wine 11 with DXMT and Apple's D3DMetal ship inside the app. No CrossOver subscription, no Homebrew, no Terminal.

Your library at a glance

A Home view shows every game and its current status: ready, playing, or needs setup.

Screenshots while you play

A shortcut captures the game window rather than the whole screen. The shots open in a viewer on the game's page, with a gallery and arrow keys.

Per-game graphics

Choose DXMT or D3DMetal, GPTK 3 or GPTK 4, window mode, and a frame-rate cap for each game independently.

GPU et mémoire en direct

A gauge and rolling graph show how hard your GPU and memory are working while you play.

Vos sauvegardes sont protégées

Removing a game asks first. It counts the save files at stake and offers to export them before anything is deleted.

Readiness checks

The game page flags issues like tight memory and offers a one-tap fix before you play.

Frame cap & FPS overlay

Cap the frame rate to cut heat and fan noise, enable MetalFX upscaling, and show a live FPS readout when you want it.

Everything included

The complete feature list

  • One-click Overwatch setup, with a clean container, Battle.net, and launch, and nothing to configure.
  • Add any Windows game, each in its own isolated container.
  • Installs from a GOG or other Windows installer, then finds the game's program for you.
  • A bundled Wine 11, DXMT, and Apple D3DMetal engine, with no CrossOver, Homebrew, or Terminal.
  • Per-game graphics, with DXMT or D3DMetal, GPTK 3 or GPTK 4, and MetalFX upscaling.
  • Per-game display mode, resolution, graphics quality, and frame-rate cap.
  • A windowed mode that lets you Cmd-Tab out during a match.
  • A live GPU, memory, and swap readout with a rolling graph as you play.
  • Readiness checks that flag problems and fix them in one tap.
  • A low-memory warning that shows which apps to close on a 16 GB Mac.
  • Frees memory automatically by closing the game's launcher once it has started.
  • A built-in performance log that makes stutter easy to trace later.
  • A Metal FPS and frame-time overlay you can turn on when you want it.
  • Clear the shader cache from Settings to reclaim disk space.
  • Cover art for every game, taken from the game's own program when it is not on Steam.
  • Drag a Windows program onto the library to add a game.
  • Repair a container to restore missing fonts and re-apply a game's settings.
  • See the disk space each game uses, and the total across your library.
  • Thirty-three languages with an in-app switcher that applies without a restart.
  • Screenshots of the game window, taken with a shortcut and shown in a built-in viewer.
  • Eight colour themes, four dark and four light, with the default drawn from the app icon.
  • Favourites that pin a game to the top of your game list and the sidebar.
  • Search by name, and sort by last played, name or size.
  • Save protection: before a game is removed, its save files are counted and can be exported.
  • Alt now reaches your games. macOS previously kept it for typing special characters.
  • Rename any game whose name the store or an archive filename got wrong.
  • Export your games, preferences and per-game settings to one file, then restore them on another Mac.
  • A warning before you download a game that needs a graphics feature neither backend provides.
  • Corvus Arcade checks a multi-part installer's files before anything runs, and says if one is missing or damaged.
  • Automatic updates you can install in one click.
  • A first-run check for Rosetta 2, the bundled engine, and free disk space.

FAQ

Common questions

Does Overwatch work on a Mac?

Yes, on Apple Silicon. Corvus Arcade installs Battle.net and Overwatch into an isolated Windows container and runs them through a bundled Wine 11 engine with Apple's D3DMetal, so no Windows licence, Boot Camp or virtual machine is involved.

Can I run Windows games on Apple Silicon without CrossOver?

Yes. The Wine 11, DXMT and D3DMetal engine ships inside the app, so there is no CrossOver subscription, no Homebrew and no Terminal commands. Download the app and press Launch.

Is Corvus Arcade free?

Yes, it is free and has no account, no telemetry and no tracking. It runs entirely on your Mac.

Which games can I add?

Any Windows game with an installer or an .exe, each in its own isolated container. Overwatch is the curated, proven title, and DRM-free GOG installers have been tested. Anti-cheat games and DirectX 12-only titles often will not run.

Do I need Rosetta 2?

Yes, and Corvus Arcade installs it for you if it is missing. The first-run system check also confirms the bundled engine and that you have enough free disk space.

Is Corvus Arcade safe to install?

Yes. Every release is signed with an Apple Developer ID certificate and notarized by Apple, which scans it for malware before it is published. macOS verifies that signature when you open the app, so you will not see an unidentified developer warning. The disk image is stapled too, so the check works even offline. Corvus Arcade collects no data and requires no account.

What Mac do I need?

An Apple Silicon Mac, meaning M1 or newer, running macOS 26 or later. Intel Macs are not supported. Battle.net plus Overwatch needs roughly 60 GB of free space.
